## Why

Slicers and file browsers don't show what's inside a 3MF. If your models live in folders on a NAS or in a synced cloud
folder, finding "that box with the hinged lid" means opening files one by one. 3MF Library reads the previews the
slicer already stored in each file and turns the folder into something you can browse, search and print from, on
your computer and on your phone.

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Previews from the files themselves" icon="seti:image">
    Bambu Studio, OrcaSlicer and PrusaSlicer store plate images in every 3MF; MakerWorld downloads add photos and a
    description. STLs and image-less 3MFs get a rendered preview.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Your folders, as they are" icon="open-book">
    No importing, no tags, no database to maintain. The folder structure on disk is the organisation; the library is
    mounted read-only.
  </Card>
  <Card title="A page per model" icon="document">
    All plates, photos, description, print time, filament and colours, and a 3D viewer you can rotate on any device.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Find things fast" icon="magnifier">
    Search names, titles, designers and part names; sort and filter by type; see everything in a kit at once.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Straight to your slicer" icon="rocket">
    Open in Bambu Studio, OrcaSlicer, Cura and more, or have your slicer open the synced original on your own computer.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Made for home servers" icon="laptop">
    One small container for amd64 and arm64. No login needed at home, accounts for everywhere else.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
